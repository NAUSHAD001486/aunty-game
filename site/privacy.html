Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Welcome to AuntyPari (accessible via https://www.auntypari.com). Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, how we safeguard it, and your rights regarding your data.

This policy has been structured to comply with global privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) for users in the United States, and relevant Indian data protection laws. [1, 2]

1. Important Notice: Age Restriction (Not for Children)

Our game is designed strictly for a mature audience and is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personal data from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it from our servers. By using our website, you confirm that you meet the minimum age requirements of your jurisdiction.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We only collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide a smooth, engaging, and personalized auntypari-style gaming experience. [1]

A. Session and Gameplay Data (Cookies & Local Storage)

  • What we collect: Temporary cookies and local storage tokens on your browser to identify your active session. [1]
  • Purpose: To track and securely save your high scores during gameplay so that your progress is not lost if the page reloads.
  • Retention: Game score records and session identifiers are stored temporarily for a period of 3 to 7 days, after which they are automatically purged or overwritten.

B. Winner’s Leaderboard Information (User-Generated Content)

  • What we collect: If you achieve a top score and become a "Champion" or "Latest Winner," you may voluntarily provide your name, nickname, or an image profile. [1]
  • Strict Consent Mechanism: We never publish your details automatically. We display your name, score, and image only if you explicitly grant permission to do so.
  • No Monetary Stakes: This is purely a recreational reward system. No lottery, real money, or financial compensation is involved.
  • Display Duration: To keep the leaderboard fresh and protect user privacy, a winner’s details are displayed in selected prominent areas of the site for a maximum of 12 hours (or less) before being cleared.

C. Automated Analytics (Google Analytics)

  • What we collect: IP addresses, browser types, device models, operating systems, and in-game behavioral data (e.g., duration of play, bounce rates). [1]
  • Purpose: We integrate Google Analytics to monitor user behavior patterns. This data helps us optimize game mechanics, fix layout glitches, and enhance the overall User Experience (UX). [1]

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

To monetize this website, we plan to implement Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses specialized tracking mechanisms to serve advertisements on our site. [1]

  • DART Cookies: Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it to serve targeted ads to our users based on their visit to auntypari.com and other websites across the internet.
  • Personalized Ads: These cookies help customize the ad units you see so they align with your regional or browsing interests.
  • Opting Out: Users can choose to decline personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings Page. Alternatively, you can block third-party vendor cookies by visiting www.aboutcookies.org or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page. [1, 2]

4. Detailed Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to ensure optimal site performance. We utilize the following categories: [1]

Cookie Type Provider Purpose Retention
Essential / Functional AuntyPari (First-Party) Maintains user session identity and stores active game high scores. 3 to 7 Days

5. International Compliance & User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Since our goal is to scale the game worldwide, we honor international data rights: [1, 2]

A. EEA and UK Users (GDPR Compliance)

If you reside within the European Union or United Kingdom, you hold the following statutory rights under the GDPR:

  • Right to Access / Portability: You can request details of any data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can demand that we delete your stored high scores or voluntary leaderboard details instantly.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can revoke your cookie or leaderboard consent at any movement without penalty.
  • Consent Mode V2: We support Google's Consent Mode V2, ensuring no tracking scripts fire for European users until active consent is received. [1]

B. California Residents (CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Under California law, users have the right to know what personal data is collected, request its deletion, and opt-out of the "sale or sharing" of personal data. [1]

  • No Sale of Data: AuntyPari does not sell, lease, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers. Data is shared only with certified processors like Google Analytics and Google AdSense for operational purposes. [1]

6. Data Security and Retention

We take administrative and technical measures to shield your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Your dynamic gameplay data is kept brief (3-7 days max) to reduce any exposure risk. Winner logs are permanently deleted from public view after their 12-hour showcase window. [1]
