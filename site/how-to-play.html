How to Play

Welcome to AuntyPari! Get ready for a thrilling, fast-paced arcade challenge that tests your timing, reflex, and focus. The gameplay is simple to learn but highly challenging to master.

Here is the complete guide on how to play, control, and score high in the game.

🎮 Game Objective

Your main goal is to control the flying character (AuntyPari) and guide her safely through a series of narrow gaps between vertical bamboo obstacles. The further you go without hitting anything, the higher your score will be!

🕹️ Game Controls (Desktop vs. Mobile)

You can enjoy AuntyPari seamlessly on both mobile phones and desktop/laptop computers. The controls are completely intuitive:

  • On Mobile / Tablets: Simply tap anywhere on the screen. Every single tap pushes the character upward.
  • On Laptops / PCs: Use your Mouse Left-Click or press the Spacebar key on your keyboard to soar upward.

📜 Gameplay Rules & Mechanics

1. Defy Gravity (The Balance):

  • One click or tap will push the character one step up.
  • Two quick clicks will push her two steps higher.
  • If you stop clicking, gravity will instantly pull the character downward toward the river/ground. You must find the perfect tapping rhythm to maintain a stable hover.

2. Avoid the Obstacles (The Zig-Zag Pattern):

  • As you move forward, bamboo pillars will appear from both the top and the bottom of the screen in a challenging, dynamic zig-zag formation.
  • You must guide the character precisely through the empty spaces between the top and bottom bamboos.

3. Game Over Conditions:

  • The Crash: If the character touches any part of the upper or lower bamboo obstacles, it’s an instant Game Over.
  • The Fall: If you stop tapping completely and the character falls all the way to the absolute bottom of the screen, you will be knocked out.

4. Scoring System:

  • Every time you successfully clear a set of top and bottom bamboo pillars, your score increases by +1 point.
  • Your live score in the top-right corner of the screen so you can track your progress.

🏆 Pro Tips for High Scores

  • Don't Panic-Tap: Instead of tapping rapidly and continuously, observe the upcoming gap and use light, timed taps to smoothly transition through the pillars.
  • Anticipate the Pattern: Keep your eyes slightly ahead of the character to see if the next bamboo opening is higher or lower, allowing you to prepare your clicks early.
  • Claim the Leaderboard: Once you score high, make sure to submit your achievement. With your consent, you could be showcased as our Champion on the homepage for up to 12 hours!
